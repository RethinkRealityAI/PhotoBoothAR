# Live wall music: licence records

Every track in this folder is a **public domain (CC0 1.0)** recording first
published on **FreePD.com**, the public domain music library founded by Kevin
MacLeod. FreePD.com has since closed. Each file was taken from the Internet
Archive's mirror of it (https://archive.org/details/freepd), then loudness-matched
to -18 LUFS (-1.5 dBTP) and re-encoded to 128 kb/s stereo MP3 by Beamwall.
The music itself is unchanged.

- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Commercial use:** allowed (paid events, marketing, anywhere).
- **Attribution:** not required. The wall still credits the artist on its Now playing line.
- Only Kevin MacLeod's own FreePD uploads and Alexander Nakarada's FreePD
  releases are used, not FreePD's mixed "Page 2" contributor uploads.
- Curated 2026-11-26.

A new track may only be added with its own record here and a matching entry in
`src/lib/wallMusic.ts` (`wallMusic.test.ts` checks the two agree).
