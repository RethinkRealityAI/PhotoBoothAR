THE 3D HEAD, MEASURED NOT REASONED: all 12 anchors were buried 2.9–8.8cm inside the bust. THREE TRAPS THAT COST TIME IN THAT SOLVER, DO NOT REINTRODUCE: (a) seeding from the global HEAD_HEIGHT_CM works on the real bust but is only calibrated for one anchor scale — a synthetic case an order of magnitude away stuck in a lopsided minimum; seed and step sizes derive from the caller's anchors. REFUTED: the audit proposed a closed-form fit (scale from ear-anchor width, translate from the argmax-z nose vertex) claiming every anchor within 0.9cm.
